Locksmith scams

What you will never see from us

Emergency locksmith services are among the sectors generating the most complaints to the French consumer protection authority (DGCCRF). Here, point by point, are the most common abusive practices and how we work instead.

Abusive practices to avoidAt Secure Locksmith Paris
  • ×

    A "from €19" price on the phone, then an invoice for several hundred euros.

    A real price given on the phone from your description, and our rates published on this website.

  • ×

    The work starts before you have signed anything.

    A written, signed quote before any work. Without your signature, nothing starts.

  • ×

    The lock is drilled or replaced even though the door was simply slammed shut.

    Non-destructive opening first. Drilling is only offered as a last resort, explained and priced.

  • ×

    Cash-only payment, with no invoice or an unreadable one.

    Card, cash or bank transfer, your choice, with a detailed invoice every time.

  • ×

    A number found on a sticker imitating the town hall or the building manager.

    An identified company: name, address, Google Business Profile and verifiable public reviews.

  • ×

    A call center that sells your call to the highest-bidding locksmith.

    A locksmith who answers the phone himself and comes in person, day or night.

All questions
How much does it cost to open a door in Paris?

A slammed door is opened for €110 incl. VAT during the day (8am–10pm), €120 during the day on weekends and public holidays, and €220 at night. The price is given on the phone, then confirmed in a written quote before any work.

Do you cover the whole of Paris?

Yes, all 20 arrondissements, from the 1st to the 20th, 24/7, public holidays included. Our locksmiths are spread across the city so they can get to you quickly.

How quickly can you get here?

About 30 minutes on average within Paris. The exact time depends on traffic and is given on the phone before the locksmith sets off.

Is the quote free?

Yes, free and with no obligation. Nothing is done without your signature, and you remain free to say no.

Do you speak English?

Yes. You can call us or message us on WhatsApp in English, and the quote and invoice are explained to you clearly.

Can my insurance reimburse me?

Often, yes: break-in, theft or loss of keys, depending on your policy. Our detailed invoice is accepted by French home insurers.

How can I pay?

By card, cash or bank transfer. You always receive an invoice.
