Questions

Emergencies: your questions

Do you really come at night and on Sundays?

Yes, 24/7, public holidays included. The night rate (10pm–8am) is given on the phone before the locksmith sets off.

How quickly can you get here?

About 30 minutes on average within Paris. The exact time depends on traffic and is given on the phone.

How much does an emergency door opening cost?

€110 incl. VAT on weekdays, €160 in the evening and on weekends, €220 at night for a slammed door. A locked door starts at €180 during the day.

What should I have ready when the locksmith arrives?

If possible, an ID or proof of address: the locksmith must make sure you are the occupant. If your documents are inside, a neighbor, the caretaker or your host can vouch for you.

Can I refuse the quote on site?

Yes. The quote is free and with no obligation: if you refuse it, nothing is done.
