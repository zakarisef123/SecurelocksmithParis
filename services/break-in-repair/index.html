After a burglary or an attempted break-in, there is only one priority: being able to lock your home again tonight. We secure your door as an emergency, then offer a lasting repair, with all the documents your insurer will ask for.

Price: free quote. The exact price is given on the phone and confirmed in a written quote before any work. Day, evening and night prices

The right steps, in order

1. Don't touch anything and call the police (17) to report it. 2. File a complaint at the police station or online: your insurer will ask for the receipt. 3. Have your door secured: we work 24/7 across Paris. 4. Notify your insurer, usually within 2 working days.

Immediate securing

Replacing a ripped-out cylinder, fitting a temporary lock, reinforcing the strike plate or frame, or boarding up if the door no longer closes. The goal: you can sleep safely at home tonight.

Repair and reinforcement

Depending on the damage: A2P multi-point lock, anti-jemmy angle bars, cylinder guard, frame reinforcement, armoring of the existing door. We provide a detailed quote and before/after photos for your insurance assessor.

Frequently asked questions

Does insurance pay for the locksmith after a burglary?

With most home insurance policies, yes, on presentation of the police report and the invoice. Ours is detailed and compliant.

Can you come before the police?

It is better to wait for the police report. If the door doesn't close at all, we can secure it temporarily while preserving evidence.

Do you install armored doors?

Yes: we armor existing doors and fit reinforcements, and we install complete armored door sets on quotation.
