Slammed shut or locked: why it matters

When a door is slammed shut, only the latch (the angled bolt that retracts when you turn the handle) is holding it. A flexible shim slid between the door and the frame is usually enough to open it. When the door is locked with a key, the deadbolts are engaged: opening it without damage is still possible in many cases, but it takes longer and sometimes requires drilling the cylinder, which is always explained and priced first.

Techniques that protect your door

Shims, picks and cylinder bypass tools: our locksmith chooses the least invasive method for your type of door. Armored doors in Parisian buildings often have specific protections, and we carry the right tools. Drilling is only a last resort, with your consent, and the cylinder is then replaced on the spot.

While you wait

Think of the building caretaker (gardien), a trusted neighbor or your letting agency: someone may have a spare key. Avoid the credit-card trick and screwdrivers: they damage the seal, the strike plate or the edge of the door, and make the job longer. If a child is inside or something is cooking on the stove, tell us on the phone and we will treat your call as a priority.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to open a slammed door in Paris?

€110 incl. VAT during the day (8am–6pm), €150 in the evening (6pm–10pm) and €220 at night (10pm–8am). For a door locked with a key, prices start at €180 during the day. The price is given on the phone, then confirmed in a written quote before we start.

Will my lock be damaged?

In the vast majority of cases, no: a slammed door opens without leaving a mark. If drilling became necessary, the locksmith would explain why and give you the price beforehand, and you would remain free to say no.

How long does it take?

Often less than ten minutes for a slammed door. An armored door or a door locked with a key can take longer.
